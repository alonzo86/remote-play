## Google code scanner and ML Kit (read on 2026-10-13)

**The TV app has none of this.** Since 2026-10-13 the `tv` preset builds from its own project (`android-tv/build`) without the scanner plugin, the Gradle dependency and the ML Kit manifest entries. Checked in `build/sababi-tv.apk`: no `com/google/mlkit`, Firebase or `QrScannerPlugin` classes, and no scanner entry in the manifest. Only the phone app (`controller`) carries the library, so the Data safety form of the TV app needs no ML Kit answers. The TV app still has AndroidX, Kotlin, JetBrains annotations and JSpecify (Apache-2.0).

Sources: <https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms> and <https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure>.

- **What it means for Sababi:** the camera image and the scanned text stay on the phone (ML Kit does not send input or output to Google). ML Kit may contact Google servers for fixes, new models and hardware information. It sends Google *metrics* about how the API works in the app.
- **Metrics listed by Google:** device information (maker, model, OS version, build, ML hardware); app information (package name, app version); performance (latency); API settings (image format, resolution); input and output size, feature version, event type, error codes. Per-installation identifiers "not intended to uniquely identify a user or physical device". Sent over HTTPS, not given to third parties. Google does not say which kind (thin or bundled) the code scanner is, so we cannot say if a device identifier is also sent.
- **What the terms ask of us:** tell the users of the app that Google processes ML Kit metrics data, as the law requires; answer the Data safety form for ourselves (Google says it is our duty); no reverse engineering of the ML Kit APIs or models. The terms do not ask for consent.
- **About note (done 2026-10-13):** `about.app_scans_with_google` now says that the picture stays on the phone, that Sababi gets only the text, and that Google receives technical data such as the phone model and the app version. The Hebrew was written by Claude and needs a native reader.
- **Play Data safety form (decided by the owner on 2026-11-08, issue #36; the owner fills the forms in the Play Console after the account exists):** (1) **Sababi: Remote (phone):** declare both *Device or other IDs* and *App info and performance (Diagnostics)* for the ML Kit metrics: collected by Google's library, not shared, encrypted in transit, purpose Analytics or App functionality. This is the cautious answer, because Google does not say whether the scanner also sends a device identifier. Not collected: name, e-mail, location, contacts, photos, camera images, account or financial data; *App activity* is not collected. (2) **The profile** (name, avatar, best scores) stays on the phone and goes only to the TV on the local network, never to a server of ours or of anyone else, so it is **not "collected"** (this matches the privacy notice, "we collect nothing"). (3) **Sababi: TV Games (TV)** is a separate Play listing with its own form: no data collected, no data shared (no scanner, no ML Kit). Read Play Console's data types guide again before you submit; this is not legal advice and was not checked against Google's pages (no web access in the session).
- **Privacy notice:** the notice is `docs/legal/privacy-notice.md` (and `.he.md`); its section 4 has the scanner paragraph with the same facts as the About note. Open: the Play Data safety form (issue #36) and the public address (issue #37).
- **iPhone:** the QR scanner of the iPhone (issue #299) is our own code on Apple's VisionKit, a system framework of iOS (iOS 16 and later). It ships no third-party code, so it has no row, no license file and no About page here (decision of the owner, 2026-11-01).
- **Phones without Play services:** no scan; the Connect screen has no scan button (issue #115) and the player types the room code, so nothing is sent to Google there.
