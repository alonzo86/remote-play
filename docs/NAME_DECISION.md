# Name decision: Sababi

Date: 2026-10-15. Status: chosen by the owner. A first register search by the owner is recorded below, and Claude checked it the same day (see "Verification by Claude"). This is not legal advice.

## Decision

The name of the app is **Sababi**. "Sababi Play" is not used as the name.
"Play" is allowed only in store text, for example the store title "Sababi: TV Games" (the chosen title).
This replaces the name Wobble (FD-26 in `docs/TV_FLOW.md`). The check of the name Wobble was removed from the repository.
The app was renamed on 2026-10-15: code, texts, app IDs, package names, APK names, the Hebrew font and the docs. The logo is a pixel face with sunglasses (option 1 of the logo review) with the word SABABI.
The Hebrew texts keep the name in Latin letters (SABABI in the upper-case texts of the app, Sababi in the legal and store texts), and QR stays QR: Alon decided this on 2026-10-30 (PR 218). A Hebrew spelling is not used.

## Why Sababi and not Sababi Play

Three reviewers looked at both names. They first disagreed and then agreed on Sababi.

- "Play" is a descriptive word for games, so it gives no trademark protection and does not remove a conflict.
- Search results for a name with "Play" are not better. The store title and keywords do that job.
- Sababi has 6 letters and Sababi Play has 11. The long name is tight on an icon label, a TV tile and the 8-bit font.
- "Open Sababi Play" can be confused with Google Play in a voice command.
- People shorten the name in speech, so the long name would give two names.
- Peers such as Kahoot, Jackbox and Roblox have one short word.

## Known risks

- **Sababa marks.** Sababi is one letter from the word Sababa, so people will read it as a respelling, and a similarity argument against it is easier to make than for a wholly new name. Claude identified the SABABA marks in classes 9, 28, 41 and 42 (see "Verification by Claude"). None of them is in game software or entertainment services, but two have wording that overlaps with games. The owner's assessment is that none of them is related to mobile apps or software that is like the app. Claude agrees for most of them (see "Assessment"). The overall risk is rated low to medium. "Play" does not remove it.
- **Sababa Toys.** The old US mark SABABA TOYS (class 28, serial 76281965) is dead (cancelled). "Sababa Group" was not found in the registers.
- **Spelling and search.** People type Sababa or Sababy. Autocorrect can change the name. Sababa food and restaurant results will appear in a search.
- **Meaning.** The name has no game meaning. The game clue is in the store title.
- **Not tested:** voice assistants, the Hebrew spelling (סבבי or סבבה), and the meaning in Arabic, Spanish, German and French. Native readers must check.

What this means:

### Apple, Google Play, domains

- Apple App Store: the search works. No Sababi app. Near names in other fields: Sababa Hebrew (education), Sababa Kindergarten Math Games (education, UK store), restaurant apps. The owner's empty result was not a failed search.
- Google Play: no Sababi, Sababa or Sababy app (the owner's note is confirmed).
- sababi.com has been registered since 2017 by an unknown owner (NameBright); it returned a 502 error. sababi.app, sababi.io, sababi.gg, sababi.tv, sababi.co.il and sababi.games looked free. sababa.com, sababa.app and sababa.io are registered, and sababa.co.il appears registered (its DNS answers). sababy.com is registered.

### Assessment

The owner's assessment, which Claude mostly agrees with: none of these marks is for mobile apps or software that is like the app.

- HWG Sababa: low. Security software and consulting.
- SABABA US 90784499: low. Physical toys and games in class 28. The overlap is only that it lists card games, logic games and puzzles.
- SABABA EUTM 012105052 (Ningbo): low to medium. Physical games and stationery, but "Games" and "Playing cards" are listed without limits, and EU offices sometimes find video games and traditional games similar to a low degree.
- SABABA EUTM 018145174 and UK00918145174 (Labarrede): low to medium. The owner's business looks like non-game SaaS (delivery and food), but offices compare the registered wording, and the first class 9 term is plain "Computer software", which covers game software on paper.

Two points that lower the risk. A registration that has gone unused for a field for five years can be challenged for non-use in the EU and UK (the Labarrede and Ningbo marks are past five years). And filing the app's own mark in classes 9, 41 and 42 only, not in class 28, avoids the toy-and-game marks.

Overall rating: low to medium. This is Claude's reading of the findings, not legal advice.

## What was and was not checked

Claude could read the USPTO, UK IPO and WIPO registers, but not the Israel IPO register (a control search failed), TMview (blocked) or EUIPO eSearch (it returned no results for marks that exist). Nothing here is clearance.
