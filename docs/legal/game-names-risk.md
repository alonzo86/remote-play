# Game names: trademark risk list

Date: 2026-11-01. Issue: #297. Status: **a list for the owner to decide on. Nothing was renamed.** The owner (Alon) chose "the agent lists the risks".

**This is not legal advice.** It is a desk check by an AI agent, not a clearance search. A lawyer or a professional search report is needed before anyone relies on it. A risk level here means "how much a closer look is worth", not a legal opinion.

No registration is claimed here beyond what the cited pages said. Every "found" below needs a check in the official register before it is used in a decision. Classes to search for all names: 9 (software), 28 (games, toys), 41 (entertainment), in the US, EU, UK and Israel.

The games are free, for family and friends, and their names are the display names. Ids and saved bests do not depend on the display name, so a rename changes the strings only (as in #82).

## Per name

English name is `registry.*` in `assets/lang/strings.csv`. The Hebrew name is the text in the app now.

### Whack-a-Mole (`registry.whack_a_mole`, Hebrew: הכו בחפרפרת)

- Class: **known brand.** The words "whack a mole" describe a type of game, and many products use them. The spelling WHAC-A-MOLE is a mark of long standing.
- Evidence: a web search listed several WHAC-A-MOLE USPTO records owned by Mattel, Inc. (arcade and amusement games, computer game software, key chains). One record (Reg. 1675229) was shown as "registered and renewed", others as cancelled (2561937, 2571554). Sources were aggregator pages: [Justia WHAC-A-MOLE](https://trademarks.justia.com/740/97/whac-a-74097672.html), [Justia Mattel list](https://trademark.justia.com/owners/mattel-inc-48391/page234). Not confirmed: which record is live now, in which classes, and outside the US.
- Risk: **high.** Our spelling "Whack-a-Mole" is the common word form and is used for the game type, but it is nearly the same as a mark that a large toy company has used for games and software, and the name is used in a store listing for a game.
- Rename candidates (display name only):
  - **Mole Pop** (Hebrew: חפרפרת קופצת)
  - **Mole Bonk** (Hebrew: בונק לחפרפרת)
  - **Meadow Moles** (Hebrew: חפרפרות באחו)
  - Pick one with "Mole" in the name; the store text already says "friendly moles pop out of their holes".

### Star Shield (`registry.star_shield`, Hebrew: מגן הכוכבים)

- Class: made-up compound of two common words. Close to the one-word mark STARSHIELD.
- Evidence: a web search found no record for the two-word mark. It found STARSHIELD, serial 74158787 (filed 1991 by BluBlocker Corporation, class 9, listed as registered; the goods were not shown), and STARSHIELD, serial 97648631 (filed 2022, published 2023, classes 39 and 42, owner not shown in the result; may be the satellite service of a large company, not confirmed). Sources: [Markinton 74158787](https://markinton.com/trademark/starshield-74158787), [Markinton 97648631](https://markinton.com/trademark/starshield-97648631). A game called Starshield also has a Steam demo ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4034090)). Not checked: whether the 1991 mark covers software, and the owner of the 2022 application.
- Risk: **medium.** Two registers show the near-identical word in class 9 and 42, and a game with that name exists on Steam. "Star Shield" was already a rename (issue #82), chosen without a search.
- Rename candidates: **Comet Guard** (שומר השביט), **Star Pilot** (טייס כוכבים; note that ASTEROIDS STAR PILOT is a registered Atari mark, so avoid it), **Space Rocks** (סלעי חלל), **Sky Rocks** (סלעים בשמיים). Best: **Comet Guard** or **Space Rocks**.

### Pin Pals (`registry.pin_pals`, Hebrew: באולינג חברים)

- Class: a name made of two words that are used for bowling toys.
- Evidence: a web search found PIN PALS, serial 78499611, Reg. 3633107 (class 28, filed 2004, shown as dead); PIN PALS, serial 74283993 (1992, abandoned); PIN PALS, serial 99142283, filed 2025 by Yoodoo Universe, L.L.C., class 28, plush and stuffed toys, shown as pending. Also a "Pin Pals Bowling Set" toy sold by Step2 in 2011 and the marks PIN PAL (76364643) and PINPALS. Sources: [Markinton 99142283](https://markinton.com/trademark/pin-pals-99142283), [Markinton 78499611](https://markinton.com/trademark/pin-pals-78499611), [Step2 blog](https://step2.com/blogs/blog/pin-pals-bowling-set-is-the-newest-spring-toy). Not checked: the pending application's current status, the PINPALS record, outside the US.
- Risk: **medium.** A toy bowling set with the same name was sold, and a live application for the same words in class 28 (toys) exists. A video game is a different good, but the field is close.
- Rename candidates: **Strike Party** (מסיבת פגיעות / מסיבת באולינג), **Bowl Buddies** (חברים לבאולינג), **Phone Bowling** (באולינג בטלפון), **Strike Squad** (חבורת הסטרייק). The Hebrew name today is already descriptive; it can stay if the English changes.

### Freeze Dance (`registry.freeze_dance`, Hebrew: ריקוד קפוא)

- Class: descriptive. This is the common name of a children's party game.
- Evidence: a web search found a FREEZE DANCE record for a party game (serial 88809720, filed 2020, JAG Games, LLC; status not shown), and a product called "Freeze Dance" for Yoto players. Sources: [Justia JAG Games](https://trademark.justia.com/owners/jag-games-llc-4052154), [Yoto](https://au.yotoplay.com/products/freeze-dance). Not checked: the status of 88809720.
- Risk: **medium.** The words describe the game, so a claim is weak, but a filed mark for a party game with the same words exists.
- Rename candidates: **Freeze Party** (מסיבת קפיאה), **Music Freeze** (קפאו למוזיקה), **Stop and Wiggle** (עצרו וזוזו).

### Banana Party (`registry.snowball_splat`, Hebrew: מסיבת בננות)

- Class: two common words.
- Evidence: a web search found no BANANA PARTY record. It found many BANANA marks in class 9, 28 and 41 (BANANA KONG, BANANA ROCK, BANANA PANIC, BANANA FOR SCALE, BANANAGRAMS PARTY EDITION). Source: [Markinton BANANA KONG](https://markinton.com/trademark/banana-kong-79143386). Not checked: any BANANA PARTY mark in the official registers, and apps with this name in the stores.
- Risk: **low to medium.** No hit was found, but the search was not complete and the field around "banana" is crowded. It needs a look in the official registers and the app stores more than a rename.
- Rename candidates if a conflict is found: **Banana Bash** (באש בננות), **Jungle Party** (מסיבת ג׳ונגל), **Monkey Munch** (נשנוש קופים).

### Paint Grab (`registry.paint_grab`, Hebrew: חטיפת צבע)

- Class: descriptive (paint, grab). Evidence: a web search found no record. Not checked in the official registers. Risk: **low.** No rename candidate needed.

### Sumo Bumpers (`registry.sumo_bumpers`, Hebrew: סומו מתנגשים)

- Class: descriptive. Evidence: a web search found no record; it found retail listings of inflatable "sumo bumper" balls (generic use). Not checked in the official registers. Risk: **low.** No rename candidate needed.

### Treasure Maze (`registry.treasure_maze`, Hebrew: מבוך האוצר)

- Class: descriptive. Evidence: a web search found no relevant record. Not checked in the official registers. Risk: **low.**

### Generic names: Bumper Cars, Air Hockey, Tennis, Mini Golf, Car Race, Sheep Herding, Tower Builder

- Class: **generic or descriptive.** These are the names of a ride, a table game, sports, a race and what the player does. No one can own the plain words for that kind of goods.
- Evidence: none searched, none needed for the plain words. Not checked: marks with a logo or a longer name (for example a mark for "Air Hockey" in a stylized form), and an app with the same name in the stores. The common-word names help nobody to find the app in a store, so a store title should not depend on them.
- Risk: **low.** The risk that remains is a look-alike use, such as a famous product with a similar name; none came up.

### ROBO (the bot in Air Hockey)

- A common word. Not searched. Risk: **low.**

### The word "asteroids" in the store text for Star Shield

- Source: `docs/legal/store-listing-draft.md`, line 35 ("blast the asteroids").
- Class: **known brand.** ASTEROIDS is a mark of Atari (issue #297 says so too).
- Evidence: a web search listed ASTEROIDS registrations owned by Atari Interactive, Inc. (serial 77021999, Reg. 3367673, class 9, 2008; serial 73335341, Reg. 1224414, 1983; ASTEROIDS STAR PILOT, Reg. 6412113; ASTEROIDS: GUNNER, Reg. 4227580). Sources: [Markinton 77021999](https://markinton.com/trademark/asteroids-77021999), [Justia Atari Interactive](https://trademarks.justia.com/owners/atari-interactive-inc-158074/). Aggregator pages, current status not confirmed.
- Risk: **medium** as a word in the listing and in keywords. The lower-case word for space rocks in a sentence is normal English use, but it is the name of a famous game that ours resembles. The in-app description already says "rocks" (`registry.desc_asteroid_blast`: "a sky full of falling rocks"); the listing says "asteroids". Using "space rocks" in the store text would remove the question (the owner decides the store text).
