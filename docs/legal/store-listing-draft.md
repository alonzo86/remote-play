# Google Play listing texts: draft

**Draft for the owner to review. Not published.** Written by Claude on 2026-10-15. These are texts for the store, not for the app, so they are not in `assets/lang/strings.csv`.

Limits of Google Play: title 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000. Play has no keyword field: it searches the title, the short description and the full description. Do not repeat words many times (Play can reject a listing for keyword stuffing).

Two listings (the recommended plan): the TV app and the controller app. Each says that it needs the other one.

Facts these texts use (checked in `README.md` and `docs/games/`): 15 games (`scripts/game/registry.gd`, 2026-10-29); 1 to 4 players (Sumo Bumpers and Bumper Cars need 2; Air Hockey plays against the bot ROBO with one phone); the phone is the controller; same Wi-Fi; no account; Android phones only for now (the iPhone is unverified, so the texts do not mention it).

### Full description, English

Sababi turns your living room into a game night. Play on your Android TV. Use your phone as the controller: tilt it, point it, and play.

You need the free Sababi: Remote app on each phone (1 to 4 players). Phones and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi.

HOW TO START
1. Open Sababi on your TV. It shows a QR code and a room code.
2. Open Sababi: Remote on your phone and scan the QR code, or type the room code.
3. Choose a game with the TV remote and play.

THE GAMES
• Treasure Maze: tilt your phone to roll your ball through the maze to the treasure.
• Car Race: steer your car along the road and be first over the line.
• Banana Party: point your phone and throw bananas at the hungry animals.
• Paint Grab: tilt to move your paint blob and cover the floor with your color.
• Sumo Bumpers: bump your friends off the platform (2 to 4 players).
• Star Shield: fly your spaceship, blast the asteroids and grab upgrades.
• Pin Pals: swing your phone to bowl, with the phone as the ball.
• Tennis: swing your phone like a racket and win the rally.
• Mini Golf: aim by turning your phone, swing to putt on funny courses.
• Whack-a-Mole: friendly moles pop out of their holes, point at them and bonk them.
• Air Hockey: slide your mallet and score goals, alone against the robot ROBO or with a friend.
• Bumper Cars (2 to 4 players): bump your friends in the rink and collect gold stars.
• Sheep Herding: steer your dog and herd all the sheep into the pen together.
• Tower Builder: take turns to drop bricks and build one tall tower together.
• Freeze Dance: wiggle your phone to the music and freeze when it stops.

Easy, Medium and Hard levels in the games, big pixel graphics you can read from the sofa, and arcade-style music. English and Hebrew.

No account. No sign-up. Made for family and friends.

### Full description, English

Sababi: Remote turns your phone into a motion controller for the Sababi party games on your Android TV.

This app needs the free Sababi: TV Games app on your Android TV or streaming box. Phone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi.

• Scan the QR code on the TV, or type the room code.
• Up to 4 phones play together.
• Tilt, point and tap to play: your phone's motion sensors do the work.
• Make your own player: a name and one of 32 pixel avatars. Your best scores stay on your phone.
• Share your player with another phone with a QR code.

No account. No sign-up.
