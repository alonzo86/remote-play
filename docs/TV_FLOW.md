# TV app flow

Status: **built**. Visual style: the 8-bit arcade style (FD-18), built in phase 1 of `docs/ARCADE_STYLE_PLAN.md` for the menus, the phone and the game shell. The game worlds are pixel worlds too (phases 2 to 4 are built, REQ-GM-47i to 47n). This file holds the decisions (FD-n)
and the requirements (REQ-FL-n) of the TV app flow. The logo image and the Android
splash image exist since 2026-10-10 (FD-21 (naming), REQ-FL-50, REQ-FL-51).
Style: ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, like the other requirements files.

## 1. Flow

```
OS splash ──> Logo screen ──> Connect controllers ──> Games ──> (Variant step) ──> Game
 (Android)     (in game)        QR, code, 4 slots      tiles      for example        │
                                      ▲                   │       marble tracks      │
                                      └──── Back ─────────┘◄──── Back / Menu ────────┘
                                      │
                                   Back: quit confirm
Settings: an OPTIONS button on the Games screen opens the Settings screen. The players badge opens the Players modal.
```

## 3. Requirements

### Splash and logo screen
- REQ-FL-50: Logo file: `assets/logo/logo.png`, PNG with a transparent background, 1600x600 px, the logo centered with at least 80 px of empty space on every side. It must read well on the black background (FD-18); pixel art fits the style. The logo screen shows it at 1200x450 px (62.5% of the screen width; the file has 4 px per logo pixel, so 3 screen pixels per logo pixel) with sharp pixels (nearest filter). The file is the mascot (a smiling blue phone with a yellow screen that sababis, white motion lines) and the word SABABI in the arcade font, yellow with a blue pixel shadow, the letters on a wave (FD-21). If the file is missing, the screen draws the text "SABABI" in the arcade font (96 px, yellow) with the line "PHONE MOTION CONTROLLER GAMES" under it.
- REQ-FL-51: The Android splash (REQ-FL-1) uses black `#000000` as the background color (`boot_splash/bg_color`). It shows `assets/logo/splash.png` (`boot_splash/image`, 432x432 px, transparent, the mascot of the logo, no filter). The mascot stays inside the middle circle of 288 px, so the round crop of Android 12 and later does not cut it.
- REQ-FL-1: The app sets the Android splash (background color and icon) in the project settings.
- REQ-FL-2: After the OS splash, the TV shows a logo screen with an animation. It lasts 2 to 3 seconds. At the first start it waits for the language choice instead (REQ-FL-60).
- REQ-FL-3: OK, or a tap on a touch screen, skips the logo screen. Back does not show it (FD-6). At the first start OK chooses the focused language and Back chooses it too (REQ-FL-60); a tap outside the two items does nothing.
- REQ-FL-4: The logo screen starts the network services (the server) while it shows, so the Connect screen is ready at once. The TV reads its LAN address each second and shows a new room code when the address changes (REQ-FL-84).

### Connect screen
- REQ-FL-5: The screen has two columns, and a third card at the other end of the row while no phone is joined; they start at the same top edge (the title is level with the top of the QR card) and sit in the middle of the screen. Left: the QR code on a white square in a double frame (REQ-FL-38), with the words "SCAN TO JOIN" (`connect.scan_to_join`) above it and the words "ROOM CODE" and the 4-letter room code below it. **Far end of the row (owner, 2026-11-06; the right in English, the left in Hebrew):** a smaller card (QR 340 px, the room code is 420 px) with the words "GET THE PHONE APP TO PLAY" (`connect.get_app`; the phone app is needed to play, owner 2026-11-06) above the QR code of the phone app (`About.APP_URL` = the page `/get/` of the website `https://alonzo86.github.io/remote-play/`, which sends each phone to its store; it works when the repository is public and Pages is on, issue #37; a quiet zone of 4 modules, whole pixels). It is for a first-time user who has the TV app and does not know that there is a phone app. It has no button and no focus (FD-17). It is on the screen only while no phone is joined: when the first phone has joined only the room code is left, so a player who scans the room code never sees two codes. The two codes are at the two ends of the row and have different sizes and labels, so that they are not mixed up; when the Sababi scanner reads the app code it says "THAT IS THE APP CODE. SCAN THE OTHER ONE." (`connect.app_code_scanned`). Whether a scanner finds the wrong code when both are in its view is checked on a device (Manual test 1). Right: the title "PLAYERS" (no player count; the slots show who joined), one line of help text, and 4 player slots (REQ-FL-8). The screen does not show the WebSocket address (FD-11).
- REQ-FL-6: The screen has no button (FD-17). A line at the bottom, centered, inside the safe area (60 px margins, RISKS D5), says "PRESS OK TO CONTINUE" (yellow, 40 px, blinking, REQ-FL-42) as soon as at least one phone has joined. Before that the line is empty, except when the TV has no network address: then it says so in `UiTheme.BAD`, without blinking. The slot list shows who has joined.
- REQ-FL-7: OK opens the Games screen when at least one phone has joined. OK does nothing before that, and nothing while the quit question is open (REQ-FL-10).
- REQ-FL-8: A phone that joins or leaves updates the slots at once. A slot is a row (no box): a square (24 px) in the slot color, "1P" to "4P", the player name in upper case, and the state word at the right: "READY" in `OK`, "LOST" in `BAD`, "WAIT" in `TEXT_INFO`. An empty slot shows a dark gray square (`SLOT_EMPTY`) and no name. The state is always a word. A phone that leaves shows "LOST" for at most 5 s (`Protocol.SLOT_GRACE_MS`), then its slot is empty again (issue #44); a phone that returns with its token in that time gets the slot back. In a game the pause card of REQ-GM-30 is the status. **Planned (issue #393, FD-30):** while the setting "approve new players" is ON, a phone that waits for approval has no slot and is not in the slot list; it gets a slot only when the host allows it (REQ-FL-94, REQ-FL-95); a returning phone with a known token gets its slot back with no card (D11).
- REQ-FL-9: A phone that is already joined stays joined when the host goes Back from the Games screen to this screen.
- REQ-FL-10: Back on this screen opens a quit confirmation (Quit, Stay). The default focus is "Stay".

### Games screen
- REQ-FL-11: The screen shows one tile for each game in `registry.gd`. A tile has a 16:9 screenshot and the game title (FD-7).
- REQ-FL-12: The tiles are in rows of 3. The content is a column of 1720 px (`tv_games.gd` `CONTENT_W`), centered on any screen width. A tile is 480 px wide (the 16:9 screenshot is 480x270 px) and keeps this size, focused or not (FD-18, question 1 of the plan: the description is in a bar under the grid, REQ-FL-13). The D-pad moves the focus in all 4 directions. The grid scrolls to keep the focused tile in view.
- REQ-FL-12a: The grid has no scroll bar. A scroll cue shows that rows are hidden (issue #220): a row of fixed height between the grid and the info bar holds two pixel arrows in `ACCENT` (up and down, 24 px high), blinking like a prompt (REQ-FL-42). The down arrow points down and is on while rows are hidden below the visible part, the up arrow points up and is on while rows are hidden above (they were drawn the wrong way round until 2026-11-02); both are off when everything fits. The row is inside the 60 px margin and the arrows are symmetric, so they are the same in Hebrew. There is no counter: the arrows give the cue and no text is needed (no new string). Tests: `tests/games_focus.gd`.
- REQ-FL-13: Every tile has the same layout: the screenshot in a 6 px frame, the title (40 px, up to 2 lines; issue #225) and the player range (32 px, dim, for example "1-4 PLAYERS") under it, so the name leads and the range stays on the tile (nothing is cut). The title of the focused tile keeps clear of the cursor: it is as wide as the tile minus the room of the cursor, and a title that is wider breaks into 2 lines (it ran under the cursor for SUMO BUMPERS in Hebrew until 2026-11-02; tests: `games_focus`). Decision of the UI/UX designer on 2026-10-30, delegated by Alon (issue #225): the range does not move to an icon row or to the description bar. The bar shows only the focused game, so a move there would take the range away from the other 14 tiles, and the Games screen is the one place where all ranges can be compared; an icon row would replace a readable text by a symbol the children and the Hebrew layout must learn. The hierarchy is already made by size (40 px title against 32 px range), color (white against the dimmer info gray) and position (title first). The frame is `TEXT_DIM`. The focused tile has an `ACCENT` frame of 12 px (twice the normal line, inside the 480x270 picture, so the tile keeps its size), an `ACCENT` title and a cursor (REQ-FL-41) before the title; only the focused tile has its title moved in to make room for the cursor (the other titles start at the edge of the tile, at the right edge in Hebrew). The description of the focused game shows in a framed bar of 3 lines under the grid (3 lines, so the bar keeps its height). A tile that does not accept the players shows the reason in yellow under its title (REQ-FL-14).

### Players modal
- REQ-FL-32: The modal shows the 4 slots with color and state, a QR code with the room code for new phones, and a Close button. It opens on the Games screen (FD-12).
- REQ-FL-33: Each joined or lost slot has a "REMOVE" item (red with the focus). Remove disconnects the phone and frees the slot.
