# Mini Golf (`mini_golf`)

Type: 2D with a tilted view from the front and above (pixel world). Players: 1 to 4. Input: swing and phone direction (the sensor of the phone).
General requirements: `docs/GAME_REQUIREMENTS.md`, also the rules for children (REQ-GM-80 to 84). Exceptions are in the first section.
Plan: `docs/GAMES_ROADMAP.md` (branch 8). Status: built on 2026-10-17 (code, tests and tile); the feel is tested on devices in Manual test 26. The look was shown in the project thread on 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-17 (mocks in the project files, `screens/mini-golf/`, the last one is `v6-*`): the course was seen from behind the golfer, in perspective, in the 8-bit world. **Redesigned on 2026-11-03** (decision of the owner, `docs/MINI_GOLF_REDESIGN.md`, option A): the whole hole is seen from above with one scale (REQ-MG-18), only the ball on turn is drawn, white (REQ-MG-2), the golfer is a small figure by the ball on turn (REQ-MG-16) and the park is a border round the course (REQ-MG-19). The simulation, the rules, the hole data and the scoring did not change, and nor did the phone controls except the strength of a swing that counts (REQ-MG-5, light swings, later the same day); the view is all in `golf_look.gd` (and the layout calls in `mini_golf_game.gd`), so that the owner can switch to option B (a follow camera) by changing the view only. The new look is checked on a TV in Test 26 steps 14d and 14e.

## Goal

Players take turns on a small golf course. On a turn the player points the phone to aim, then swings the phone back and through like a real putt. The
swing speed sets the power. Each player putts once, then the next player (slot order) until every ball is in the hole. The player with the fewest strokes over
all holes wins. With 1 player the game is practice against the par of each hole.

Registry entry: `description` (one line) and `tile` (`assets/tiles/mini_golf.png`, made by `tools/make_tiles.gd`), see REQ-GM-7.
Visual language: shell, player colors, pixel world (REQ-GM-45 to 49). Check the tile next to the Games screen.

## Open questions

- The shared detector (`scripts/input/swing_detector.gd`, from Pin Pals, used by Tennis since 2026-10-17) gives `aim_locked` from the start of the back swing and `power` at the release. Mini Golf needs only these two. Issues #141 and #142 (2026-10-29) did not change Mini Golf: the new `fwd_ratio` rule of the detector is off here, because a putt is a pendulum whose forward swing can be as soft as its back swing (the Mini Golf tests use a forward swing of 8.6 after a back swing of 8), and the thresholds stay as they were. Issue #209 (2026-11-01) then added the speed gate (`speed_gate`, REQ-MG-5): the hand must really move forward at the push, which a sweep or a slow shake does not do, while a pendulum putt that brakes its back swing does (the Mini Golf tests now use a hand-like swing, `SensorReplay.pendulum()`, because a forward swing of 8.6 after 8 with no brake is the same series as a sweep). `fwd_ratio` stays off. 0.5 m/s is a first guess; a very hard shake or a very fast sweep (about 25 m/s2) still putts, and a soft putt may be dropped: Test 26 steps 16h and 16i. It needs `lock_after` and `threshold` set for putts (a putt swing is slower than a throw or a stroke); the first values are guesses and are tuned on a device (Manual test 25 for Tennis, a new test for Mini Golf with the code).
