Games on your TV.

Your phone is the controller. Tilt it, point it, swing it, and play together on the big screen.

How it works

  1. Install "Sababi: TV Games" on your Android TV.
  2. Get the phone app ("Sababi: Remote"). You need it to play.
  3. Start "Sababi: TV Games" on your Android TV.
  4. Open the phone app and join the room.
  5. Pick a game on the TV and play!

Get the TV app

The TV app, "Sababi: TV Games", runs on Android TV.

Get the phone app

You need the phone app to play: the TV shows the games and the phone is the controller.
