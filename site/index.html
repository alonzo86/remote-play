Party games on your TV.

Your phone is the controller. Tilt it, point it, swing it, and play together on the big screen.

How it works

  1. Get the phone app (Sababi: Remote). You need it to play.
  2. Start Sababi: TV Games on your Android TV. The TV shows a code.
  3. Open the phone app and scan the code. Pick a game on the TV and play!

Get the phone app

You need the phone app to play: the TV shows the games and the phone is the controller. The app is free.

Google Play App Store

The TV app, Sababi: TV Games, runs on Android TV.
