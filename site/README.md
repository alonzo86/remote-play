# Sababi website

The pages of https://alonzo86.github.io/remote-play/ (GitHub Pages, deployed from this folder by `.github/workflows/pages.yml`).

| Page | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `index.html`, `he/index.html` | Home (English, Hebrew right to left) with the section "Get the phone app" and the store buttons |
| `get/index.html` | The page that the QR code "GET THE PHONE APP TO PLAY" of the TV opens (`About.APP_URL`). A phone goes straight to its own store when the address is filled in |
| `privacy.html`, `terms.html` (and `he/`) | **Generated** from `docs/legal/privacy-notice*.md` and `terms*.md` by `tools/make_legal_pages.py` (run it after a change of the texts; `tests/legal_pages.gd` fails when a page is out of date). They show the text as it is. The line "Draft, not published" was removed from the four texts on 2026-11-09 (owner), and the "Last updated" date is 2026-11-09. Not published: GitHub Pages is off until the owner turns it on (issue #37) |
| `favicon.ico`, `icon-192.png` | The Sababi icon of the tab (the app icon `assets/icons/icon_1024.png`, our own artwork; owner request of 2026-11-09). Every page links them in its `<head>`; `tests/site_language.gd` checks it |
| `screens/` | The store screenshots of the home page: one carousel line under the description (10 for the TV, 2 for the phone; no title of their own; owner request of 2026-11-09), copies of `docs/store-assets/google-play/` at half size (960 x 540 and 540 x 960, averaged down by 2). English only: the Hebrew home page shows the same pictures with a Hebrew description of each. The line scrolls and snaps (`carousel.js` adds the two arrow buttons beside the line, not over the pictures, and hides the scrollbar; without JavaScript there are no buttons and the scrollbar stays; the buttons have the texts Previous / Next and הקודם / הבא). When a store screenshot is captured again, make its copy again |
| (no licenses page) | The Licenses tab and its placeholder page were removed on 2026-11-09 (owner): the license notices are required in the apps, and they are on the About screens and packed in every export (`docs/THIRD_PARTY.md`). Add a page only if a store asks for a web address of the licenses |
| `lang.js`, `flags/us.svg`, `flags/il.svg` | The language menu of the header (a `<details class="lang">` in every page, English and עברית, each with a flag; owner request of 2026-11-09, issue #37). It works without JavaScript; `lang.js` closes it on a click outside, on Escape and after a choice. The two flags are simple SVG drawings made for this project (no third-party artwork). To add a language: one more `<li>` in the menu of every page and one more flag file; `tests/site_language.gd` checks the 6 pages |
| `badges/` | The official Google Play badge (English and Hebrew), **not our artwork**: use it as published, never edit it, keep the credit line of the footer of the home page, and keep its row in `docs/THIRD_PARTY.md` ("Website store badges"). There is **no App Store badge** (removed on 2026-11-09, owner: Apple allows its badge only for an app that is available): add it back, with its row and credit line, when the iOS app is published (the issue about the iOS release steps) |
| `stores.js` | The two Google Play addresses (phone app and TV app; the home page has a section for each app, the TV app first). They are made from the package ids and work when the apps are published. An empty address shows the badge without a link. There is no App Store address |
| (footer) | Every page's footer names the provider and the contact and has the line "© 2026 Alon Yampolski. All rights reserved." (Hebrew: "© 2026 אלון ימפולסקי. כל הזכויות שמורות."; owner decision of 2026-11-09, the Hebrew approved by the owner). The home pages add the store trademark credits. It matches `LICENSE` (all rights reserved). The credits that `CLA.md` promises to contributors are in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`; the website has no credits place yet (add one, in English and Hebrew, when the first contributor is credited) |
| `style.css` | The look, with the colors of the design tokens (`docs/design-system/tokens.css`) |

Rules:
- The address of `get/` is drawn by every installed TV app (`scripts/core/about.gd`, `APP_URL`): never move it. Change where it sends people in `stores.js`.
- The store badge (Google Play) is trademarked artwork: use only the official file and follow the brand rules of the store, and add any new badge to `docs/THIRD_PARTY.md` first.
- The privacy notice, the terms and the store texts are owner only (CLAUDE.md). Edit `docs/legal/*.md`, never the generated pages by hand.
- To see it: `cd site && python3 -m http.server 8000`.
